<h1 align="center">Cue</h1>
<p align="center">A teleprompter that floats over Instagram, Snapchat, TikTok and your camera.</p>
<p align="center"><a href="https://khanhamzahk771-collab.github.io/cue-teleprompter/"><b>Open Cue →</b></a></p>

---

### Install on iPhone

1. Open the link above in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Launch **Cue** from your home screen.

### Record with it

1. Paste your script and pick a speed and text size.
2. Tap **Make floating prompter**, then **Float over other apps**.
3. Open your camera app, drag the prompter under the front camera and press play.

The prompter keeps scrolling while you record, and it never appears in your video.

### Privacy

Everything runs on your phone. Scripts are never uploaded or stored anywhere else.
