Record on Instagram, Snapchat or Camera

  1. Tap Float over other apps. A small prompter window pops out.
  2. Swipe up to go home and open Instagram, Snapchat, TikTok or Camera.
  3. Drag the prompter next to your front camera and pinch it bigger.
  4. Tap it and press play. It counts down 3-2-1, then scrolls. Hit record: it keeps scrolling while you film.
  5. If it ever stops when you hit record, tap the window once and press play again.

The prompter won't show up in your video: camera apps record the camera, not your screen. Swipe it to the screen edge to tuck it away, and tap the arrow to bring it back.
